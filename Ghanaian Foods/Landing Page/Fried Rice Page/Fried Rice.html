Origin

Fried rice originated in China during the Sui Dynasty (581–618 CE) in the city of Yangzhou, Jiangsu province. It was created as a practical way to reuse leftover cold rice and prevent food waste.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Aromatics
  • Vegetables
  • Protein
  • Sources and Oils
  • Seasoning

Procedure

  1. Cook long-grain rice, spread it onto a tray, and chill in the fridge overnight.
  2. Heat oil in a wok or large pan over high heat, scramble eggs, and set aside.
  3. Saute diced onions, garlic, and ginger until fragrant, then toss in mixed vegetables and protein.
  4. Add the chilled rice to the pan, breaking up clumps while stirring over high heat.
  5. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil, then season with white pepper and salt.
  6. Fold in the scrambled eggs and chopped scallions, tossing constantly until everything is hot.
